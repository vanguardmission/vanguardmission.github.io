About us

A missionary family sent to the Nations

We belong to Jesus Christ and take part in what He is doing to make His name known where there is still no witness. The calling was born in the backlands of northeast Brazil and has always had one focus: standing beside those who serve.

We do not look for momentary decisions but for transformed lives: people who are discipled, stand firm in the faith and belong to local Churches that grow and multiply.

Where we are

Our base is in northeast Thailand, among the Isan people, who still have limited access to the Gospel. From there we also reach a neighboring country where religious freedom is restricted and the Suffering Church stands firm.

For the safety of our brothers and sisters, we do not publish names, faces or exact locations.

What we do

Three fronts, one calling

Evangelism and discipleship

Proclamation

Regular evangelism in villages with very little Christian presence, looking for “houses of peace” (Luke 10) to disciple new believers and establish local Churches.

Partnership with the Light Athletics outreach center, which reaches children, youth and their families through sport, and presence on university campuses.

A continual altar

Prayer and Intercession

With missionaries and local leaders we keep a continual place of worship and Prayer. Every true transformation begins with God.

We intercede for the Suffering Church, for native workers and for their families.

The Gospel in action

Real Support

Daily meals and care for about 40 children and youth at a mission base, with discipleship and conversions.

Support for believers facing persecution in isolated regions, care for missionary and worker kids, and a fish-farming project that strengthens a village.
